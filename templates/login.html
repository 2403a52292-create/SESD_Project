About Us

ExpiryGuard is a smart inventory management system designed to help businesses manage products, monitor expiry dates and maintain accurate stock information.

The system brings inventory tracking, expiry monitoring, sales recording and stock management together in one organized platform.

Our goal is to help businesses reduce product wastage, improve inventory visibility and make better stock management decisions.

Platform Overview

Administrator

Manages system access, oversees inventory operations and monitors overall system activity.

HR

Supports authorized staff administration and related organizational activities.

Staff

Handles permitted day-to-day inventory, stock and sales activities.

Key Differentiators

Expiry Monitoring: Helps identify products that are approaching expiry or have already expired.

Inventory Visibility: Provides organized product and batch information to support stock management.

Sales Management: Supports sales recording and inventory updates.

Inventory Insights: Helps users understand expiry risks and potential stock losses.

Role-Based Access: Organizes system access according to assigned user permissions.

Our Objective

ExpiryGuard aims to support more efficient inventory operations by improving product tracking, reducing avoidable wastage and making stock information easier to manage.
